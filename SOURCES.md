# Sources and verification policy

## Purpose

This repository provides a general payment-method taxonomy and a repeatable way to verify payment claims. The taxonomy is not evidence that an operator accepts a method. No casino-specific availability, fees, limits, currencies, or processing times should be published without an appropriate source.

## Source hierarchy

For an operator-specific claim, prefer:

1. The operator's own cashier, payment, withdrawal, and terms pages.
2. The official payment provider's documentation for the service being described.
3. Official regulator or government material for licensing and legal requirements.

For research relating to Sweden, these official Swedish Gambling Authority resources are useful starting points:

- [Online casinos — Spelinspektionen](https://www.spelinspektionen.se/spelare/spelform/onlinekasino/)
- [Spelpaus.se — Spelinspektionen](https://www.spelinspektionen.se/lagar-regler/spelansvar-och-konsumentskydd/spelpaus.se)
- [Responsible gambling — Spelinspektionen](https://www.spelinspektionen.se/spelare/spelansvar/)

These official resources support Sweden-related licensing and safer-gambling context; they do not verify the payment methods offered by an individual casino.

## What every factual claim needs

Record the exact claim, operator, country or region, source URL, and date checked. Include currency and account-eligibility limitations where relevant. Re-check time-sensitive details periodically and whenever the operator or provider changes its terms.

Do not infer that a method is supported because it is common in the country. Do not infer that deposits and withdrawals use the same method. Do not claim that a payout is instant unless the official source defines what stage the time refers to. Distinguish operator fees from payment-provider, bank, conversion, and network costs.

When the source does not answer a question, use a value such as “not stated” or “not verified.” Do not substitute assumptions, competitor claims, search snippets, or autogenerated summaries for evidence.

## Editorial and commercial transparency

Disclose commercial relationships relevant to the content. Where the maintainer owns or operates a site mentioned in this repository, identify that relationship rather than presenting the link as an independent recommendation. Links should provide context for readers and should not be the main reason the resource exists.

## Limitations

This material is general information, not financial or legal advice. Payment services and gambling rules vary by provider, country, account, and time. Readers should check current primary sources and the rules that apply to them.
