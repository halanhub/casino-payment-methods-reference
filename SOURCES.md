# Sources and verification policy

This repository intentionally gives general descriptions of payment-method categories rather than claiming that a particular casino supports a particular service.

Before adding an operator-specific claim, verify it against current primary sources, such as:

1. The operator's own cashier, payments, terms, and withdrawal pages.
2. The official website and documentation of the relevant payment provider.
3. Applicable regulator or government guidance for regional rules.

Record the country or region, the exact claim, the official source URL, and the date checked when maintaining operator-specific data. Re-check claims periodically: payment availability, supported countries, fees, limits, currencies, and processing times can change.

Do not infer that a method is supported merely because it is common in a region. Do not describe a deposit method as a withdrawal method unless the operator's terms explicitly confirm that use. Clearly distinguish provider processing time from operator review or payout time.

## General cautions

- A payment method may be unavailable due to country, currency, bank, issuer, account status, or verification requirements.
- Deposits and withdrawals may use different methods or limits.
- Fees, exchange rates, network costs, and processing times may apply.
- Cryptocurrency transfers can be irreversible and prices can fluctuate.
- This reference is informational, not financial or legal advice.
